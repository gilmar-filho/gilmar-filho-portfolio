About me

Simple systems
that actually work

Hi, I'm Gilmar Filho, an Information Systems student at UFLA (Federal University of Lavras), now in my 7th semester. I build automations for people who want their time back by handing repetitive tasks over to a system.

My specialty is turning manual processes into automated workflows — using Google Sheets with Apps Script, Python, and integrations with tools people already use every day, like Telegram.

Every project follows a deliberate choice: deliver what solves the problem well, not what impresses through volume. A system with 3 features the user relies on every day is worth more than a "complete" system that ends up as dead weight.
